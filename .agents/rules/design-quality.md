# Design quality rules

Authority: project-specific [`DESIGN.md`](../../docs/project/DESIGN.md).

- Follow the project’s creative thesis, typography, density, image grammar, and responsive art direction.
- Avoid generic SaaS composition and interchangeable marketing layouts.
- Do not default sections to cards.
- Do not spread glassmorphism, gradients, shadows, or rounded containers everywhere.
- Implement and browser-test both required signature moments.
- Borrow principles from references; never copy a reference literally.
- Perform a final visual-cohesion review across every route and viewport.
